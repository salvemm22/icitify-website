Why Icitify

We do not just teach. We launch careers.

Many young Nigerians finish school without a single practical digital skill. Icitify exists to close that gap with free, hands-on training that leads to jobs, freelance income and further study.

Our method is simple: small classes, real projects, mentorship and a certificate you can show employers. We measure success by what students do after graduation, not just attendance.

  • 100% free for students
  • Project-based learning
  • Mentorship and career support
  • Certificates of completion
  • Laptops provided in class
  • Alumni community network
